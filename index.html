FAQ

What was your typical investment amount?

We typically made an initial investment of $100 in early-stage projects.

Did you take equity?

It depended on the project. We were flexible and worked with founders to find the best arrangement.

Why did Go Human Fund close?

The fund was personally financed and we didn't pursue raising capital from limited partners. Despite this, we're proud of the impact we made and the continued success of our founders.

A Note from the Founder

Due to financial constraints and the challenges of securing long-term support, I made the difficult decision to conclude the fund's operations. While I personally financed much of our journey and learned valuable lessons about sustainable fundraising, I am profoundly grateful for the remarkable individuals and projects we were able to support.

My experiences since, including work on the Truffle AI Computer and other ventures, have only reinforced my belief in the importance of nurturing emerging talent in technology. Although the Go Human Fund is no longer active, my commitment to mentoring and uplifting the next generation of innovators remains unwavering.

With sincere appreciation,
Jacob Valdez
